ARCHIVE / STORY

STORY

THE ESTABLISHED HISTORY OF THE GAME.

The Game is an ongoing multiplayer event taking place on an island.

Across its different editions, players have encountered strange events, supernatural entities, ancient structures and increasingly dangerous threats.

The story currently spans four major chapters: Halloween 2025, Easter 2026, Summer 2026 and Halloween 2026.

HALLOWEEN 2025 — THE FIRST ENCOUNTER.

During Halloween 2025, the players encountered the Hallow King.

This was the first established appearance of the Hallow King within The Game.

No deeper lore is currently established for the events of Halloween 2025.

His origin, the history of the Hallowed Realm and the events that would follow were not established during this chapter.

EASTER 2026 — THE FOUNTAIN.

During Easter 2026, the island encountered another supernatural threat.

The Killer Bunny entered the island through the Fountain.

The Fountain has the strongest known connection between the island and the Hallowed Realm.

After entering the island, the Killer Bunny targeted the Forest Beacon.

The Forest Beacon would later be changed into the Volcano.

By Halloween 2026, the Forest Beacon no longer exists as a beacon.

SUMMER 2026 — THE FALL OF THE SEALS.

During Summer 2026, the Crimson Lord appeared within the Crimson Forest.

At the same time, the island's Seal system began to collapse.

The system included seven Seal Statues, alongside other established components such as the Beacons and the Cathedral's seals.

The destruction of the seven Seal Statues happened throughout the Summer storyline.

Two of the seven Seal Statues were destroyed.

A third Seal Statue then began breaking, and the destruction continued throughout the remainder of Summer.

Near the Summer finale, the final Seal Statue was destroyed.

ALL SEVEN SEAL STATUES ARE GONE.

By the end of Summer, all seven Seal Statues had been destroyed.

The Seal system was therefore already gone before the beginning of Halloween 2026.

THE SEVEN SEALKEEPERS.

More than 100 years ago, seven Sealkeepers attempted to create the Seal system.

Six of the original seven Sealkeepers are gone.

One remains.

The current Sealkeeper is the last of the seven and is associated with the Sealkeeper Tower.

The Sealkeeper's established history is connected to the creation of the Seal system.

THE HALLOW KING AWAKENS.

The Crimson Lord woke the Hallow King.

By the beginning of the full Halloween 2026 event, the Hallow King is already awake.

The island enters Halloween without the Seal system that once existed.

The former Forest Beacon is now the Volcano.

The Cathedral becomes one of the central locations of the Halloween storyline.

THE CATHEDRAL.

The Cathedral is the prison where the Hallow King was held.

The Cathedral Beacon is connected to the Hallow King's awakening.

If the Cathedral Beacon fails, the Hallow King awakens.

Within the Cathedral is an altar protected by four seals.

During Halloween 2026, the altar eventually shatters.

Its destruction reveals a crack into the Hallowed Realm.

THE HALLOWED REALM.

The Hallowed Realm is separate from the normal world containing the island.

It is the realm ruled by the Hallow King.

The Fountain remains the strongest known connection between the island and the Hallowed Realm.

With the Cathedral altar shattered and a connection to the Hallowed Realm revealed, the boundary between the two worlds becomes increasingly important.

THE HALLOW KING'S OBJECTIVE.

The Hallow King does not simply want to escape from the Hallowed Realm.

He wants to rule both worlds.

To achieve this, he seeks to merge the normal world and the Hallowed Realm.

This merging is known as the Convergence.

THE CONVERGENCE IS COMING.
HALLOWEEN 2026 — THE THREAT.

Halloween 2026 begins with the Hallow King already awake and the Seal system already gone.

The Killer Bunny returns during the full Halloween event.

The Crimson Lord also returns.

The events of Easter and Summer are now connected to the threat facing the island.

The players become directly involved in the conflict as the Hallow King's plan moves toward completion.

The Convergence is no longer a distant possibility.

TWO WORLDS. ONE CONVERGENCE.
THE FINAL CHAPTER IS CLASSIFIED.

What happens when the Convergence reaches its final stage has not yet been revealed.

The events of the final confrontation remain classified.

The outcome of the conflict has not been disclosed.

THE STORY CONTINUES.

Quest 10 is the final confrontation of The Game 2026.

The Hallowed Convergence begins as the Hallow King attempts to complete the connection between the island and the Hallowed Realm.

THE HALLOWED REALM IS HERE.

The players must activate and defend three Severing Anchors while the Hallow King and his forces attempt to stop them.

The battle escalates through multiple phases as the Hallow King, the Killer Bunny and the Crimson Lord enter the confrontation.

What remains after the Severing determines what comes next.
